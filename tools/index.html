When a Dedicated Tool Beats the Calculator

The main calculator handles anything you can express as a single line of arithmetic. These pages exist for the questions that are several steps long, where the steps themselves are where mistakes happen: reducing a fraction, finding a common denominator, working a percentage backwards from a discounted price.

Each tool shows the intermediate working rather than only the result, which matters when the method is what is being marked.

All of It Runs on Your Device

Every tool here is JavaScript executing in your browser. Nothing you type is uploaded, there is no account, and each page keeps working if the connection drops after it has loaded.

A free calcsolver that runs entirely in your browser: a full scientific calculator, focused math tools, and short guides. Nothing to install, no account to make.
